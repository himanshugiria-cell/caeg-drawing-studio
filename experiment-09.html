The rules you already know.

Front view uses height above HP. Top view uses distance in front of VP. Each corner’s two views share a projector perpendicular to XY. Join a′b′c′d′ for FV and abcd for TV.

A plane has length and breadth. We neglect thickness. When its surface is parallel to a reference plane, the projection there shows true shape. When perpendicular, it shows an edge view.

OFFICIAL MANUAL / WORKED CONSTRUCTIONS

One step at a time.

The manual’s square remains parallel to HP throughout its three cases. Its pentagon is an extension: after the last turn, its surface is inclined to both reference planes.

    A side angle and a surface angle are different. In square case (b), one side is at 30° to VP while the surface stays parallel to HP.

    Pentagon checks and why the last step works

    The initial perpendicular span from side ab to vertex d is 46.17 mm. Use that span as the compass radius to reach the 30 mm height locus. The resulting surface tilt is about 40.53°. After tilting, rotate the whole intermediate TV until ab makes 30° with XY. Preserve that projected shape. Then transfer the heights: a′ and b′ on XY, c′ and e′ at 18.54 mm, and d′ at 30 mm.

    The manual does not specify the distance from VP, so the drawings use a convenient gap. Mirrored arrangements can also satisfy the stated data.

    PENCIL-AND-PAPER PRACTICE

    Your turn at the drawing sheet.

    1. A 30 mm square is parallel to HP, 15 mm above it, with one side parallel to VP. Draw its two views.
    2. Repeat with all sides equally inclined to VP.
    3. A 40 mm square rests on HP on a side perpendicular to VP. Its surface is 60° to HP. Draw its views.
    Check your answers

    1. TV is a 30 mm square. FV is a 30 mm horizontal line 15 mm above XY.

    2. TV sides are 45° to XY. FV span is 42.43 mm, still 15 mm above XY.

    3. FV is a 40 mm edge view at 60°. TV is 20 mm by 40 mm. The raised corners are 34.64 mm above HP.

    Quick viva

    When does TV show true shape? Surface parallel to HP.

    When is FV an edge view? Surface perpendicular to VP.

    What is the trace of a plane? Its line of intersection with a reference plane, extending the surface if needed. HT is on HP and VT is on VP.

    Can both views be edge views? Yes, if the plane is perpendicular to both HP and VP.
